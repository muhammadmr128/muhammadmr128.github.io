When a watershed burns

Ash and sediment from boreal fires reach the rivers towns drink from. Utilities need to know when water quality shifts after a fire, and why.

How the model was built

837samples

Collect long-term monitoring data

Pulled 26 years (2000 to 2026) of water-quality records from four stations in Alberta's Peace-Athabasca Basin, a boreal watershed hit by the 2016 Fort McMurray and 2023 fires.

Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, pandas, seaborn, feature importance, ensemble ML, hydrology, water quality, environmental risk.

What the model found

Nitrogen, colour and metals drive turbidity: the chemical signature of wildfire runoff.

Figures 1 to 4: project results on the Peace-Athabasca dataset (823 samples, 80/20 train/test split). Select any figure to open it full size.

Same model, a different river

I ran the Alberta model on Labrador's Churchill River, near the 2024 Churchill Falls fires. It over-predicted by about 17×.

243NTU predicted
14NTU measured

The Churchill is hydro-regulated, tidal at the mouth and carries far less metal, and Labrador has only a handful of grab samples to retrain on. The method transfers; the fitted model has to be retrained on local hydrology. The next version predicts turbidity from what the province measures well: daily discharge, water level and fire timing.

Combined influence of modified recycled concrete aggregate and metakaolin on high-strength concrete production: Experimental assessment and machine learning quantifications with advanced SHAP and PDP analyses

Journal article

Sobuz, M. H. R., Rahman, M. M., Aayaz, R., Al-Rashed, W. S., Datta, S. D., Safayet, M. A., Kabbo, M. K. I., & Abdullah, M.

Construction and Building Materials, 461, 139897

60.36MPa from recycled aggregate
Key findings

Treated recycled concrete aggregate with metakaolin produced high-strength concrete reaching 60.36 MPa, matching conventional mixes while replacing a large share of natural aggregate with demolition waste. XGBoost (R² = 0.924) predicted strength, and SHAP and PDP analyses showed which mix factors drive it.
